Why the prices look like this

A jacket that costs more to make, costs less over ten years.

Most clothing is priced to be replaced. We price ours to be worn for a decade — heavier cloth, reinforced seams, and a repair service that's free for as long as you own the piece. It costs more up front. It's cheaper by year three.

See how we build a garment

Three fibers, no filler.

Cotton

Grown without synthetic pesticides, woven into a heavy canvas that softens over years of wear instead of thinning out.

Wool

Left undyed where we can, sourced from farms we've worked with since 2019, spun for warmth without bulk.

Leather

Tanned with tree bark and minerals rather than chromium, so it wears in — and eventually biodegrades — instead of cracking.
