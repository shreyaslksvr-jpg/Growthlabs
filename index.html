"We stopped calling it activewear the day we realized most of it never left the office." — Founding note, 2016

Underbrush started as a two-person pattern shop making shells for a trail-running club that kept complaining about everything on the market. Nine years later, the brief hasn't changed: make it work in real weather first, and let it look good as a result of that, not instead of it.
